The design rule

Players should understand Crownfall after crossing the plaza once. Everyday services sit in the center. Faction identity grows stronger toward the corners. Travel follows the north–south civic spine. Every destination earns its room.

One service heart

Everything routine is adjacent

Shop, mail, dailies, gear, vault, Codex, journal, records, skills, social tools, and travel are all on the plaza or one doorway away.

Consolidate by player intent

One trip for one job

The forge, lapidary, gear screen, and personal vault now share Artisans' Court. Kitchen and Alembic share the Hearthworks. Duplicate gear rooms are gone.

Landmarks before litter

Rooms have a composition

Each room has an exact landmark and interaction. Furniture is deliberately placed, legacy prop scatter is suppressed, and every prominent structure now exposes either a real service or a physical-purpose inspection.
