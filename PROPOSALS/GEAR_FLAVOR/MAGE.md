# Gear Flavor — Mage

The Mage is the Vale's arcane scholar: an archmage who reads magic as a body of
LAWS — axioms, wards, the wheel of stars, the deep firmament and the worldroot
under everything. Her power, like every bearer's, is a shard of Vargoth's will;
she is the one who tries to write it back into rule. Uniques get one line, keyed
by name, echoing their passive. Generic shapes get FOUR grade-banded lines each —
the same piece growing with its grade, the way the gem level-bands do — keyed by
shape name plus grade band (F–E crude, D–C serviceable, B–A masterwork, S peak).

|---|---|
| F–E | A clouded glass bauble that hums faintly and cracks if looked at wrong, more curio than focus. |
| D–C | A standard focus-sphere of clouded glass that hums when a spell is near, so scholars feel the arcane and not only cast it. |
| B–A | A masterwork orb, its glass clear at the core, thrumming in tune with every current of the arcane around her. |
| S | An orb that answers a thought before she voices it; the arcane does not pass near it so much as through it. |

#### Starshard
| Grade band (key) | Flavor |
|---|---|
| F–E | A dull chip of star-slag, cold to the touch and dim, keeping only a memory of the fall. |
| D–C | A serviceable starshard, still cold with the distance it crossed, lending a bolt some of that long killing patience. |
| B–A | A fine shard that has never once warmed, its old star-cold sharpening every spell it rides. |
| S | A splinter of a star still burning somewhere far off; the cold in it is the patience of something that will outlast the fight. |

#### Aegis Crystal
| Grade band (key) | Flavor |
|---|---|
| F–E | A flawed warding-crystal, cloudy and chipped, shedding a spell about as well as a cracked roof sheds rain. |
| D–C | A standard aegis crystal cut to shed a spell the way a roof sheds rain, kept close for the blow the robe won't catch. |
| B–A | A masterwork crystal faceted clean, turning aside a strike as smoothly as glass turns light. |
| S | A crystal that admits nothing; the blow meant for her simply finds the surface and is sent elsewhere. |

#### Zephyr Sigil
| Grade band (key) | Flavor |
|---|---|
| F–E | A crudely scratched wind-rune that tugs a little and lies a lot about where the gap really is. |
| D–C | A serviceable zephyr sigil, a rune of moving air always a half-beat ahead, tugging her toward the gap before she sees it. |
| B–A | A fine sigil that reads the currents true, steering her clear a full breath ahead of the blow. |
| S | A sigil that is the wind's own intent; she is already gone where it points before the strike arrives. |

#### Lifebloom Pendant
| Grade band (key) | Flavor |
|---|---|
| F–E | A withered seed clouded in cheap resin, its green nearly spent, answering frost with a weak flush and no more. |
| D–C | A standard lifebloom pendant, a seed of green sealed in amber, the battle-mage's small argument against dying. |
| B–A | A masterwork pendant whose sealed bloom answers every frost with a surge of stubborn green. |
