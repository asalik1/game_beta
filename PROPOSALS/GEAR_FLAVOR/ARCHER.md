# Gear Flavor — Archer

The huntress of the Ashvale wilds: wind, distance, patience, and the beast-world
of the Wildfang tribes and the old Stormwarden bloodline. Her power is a shard of
Vargoth's will, carried the way a good hunter carries a debt. Format per the shared
brief — each generic shape carries FOUR grade-banded lines (F–E / D–C / B–A / S) so
the piece grows with its grade, keyed by shape name + band; uniques are keyed by
exact name, each line echoing its passive.
