# Gear Flavor — Paladin

The oath-bound arbiter of the fallen kingdom: light, judgment, the vow. His Ember shard rides as a
chain around the heart that argues its own verdicts, and his dark foil is the Hollow Choir — a faith
turned to Mórwyn's rot. Faith checks the guilty the way steel checks a lesser man. Flavor keyed by
exact item name (the in-game lookup key). Generic shapes now grow with their grade — four flavor lines
each, banded F–E / D–C / B–A / S, the same piece rising in quality; uniques echo their passive.
