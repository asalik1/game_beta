# Gear Flavor — Warrior

The Warrior is a soldier of the fallen kingdom of Emberfall: steel, banners, the last
charge, discipline. His power is a shard of Vargoth's will, and much of his best gear
comes off the **Crownless Host** — the army that kept fighting after there was no crown
left to serve. Flavor keyed by exact item name (the in-game lookup key). Generic shapes
now grow with their grade across four quality bands (F–E crude, D–C issue, B–A masterwork,
S exceptional) — the same shape rising from militia scrap to something exceptional; uniques
echo their passive.
