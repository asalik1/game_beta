# Gear Flavor — Assassin

Per the shared brief (`PROPOSALS/GEAR_FLAVOR/BRIEF.md`). The Assassin (they/them) is a phantom of
shadow and poison — silence, the knife in the dark, and a Crown-shard that *takes to keep them alive*:
the blood surge is stolen warmth, the flask emptied and the carter left grey. Their line remembers the
first of their kind, who was killed once and declined. Generic flavor is grade-banded — the SAME shape at
rising quality across four bands (F–E crude/scavenged, D–C issue-grade, B–A masterwork, S peak), so a
piece grows with its grade. Unique flavor echoes the item's passive (weapons:
`PROPOSALS/GEAR_UNIQUE_PASSIVES.md` §5; armor slots: `PROPOSALS/GEAR_ARMOR_UNIQUE_PASSIVES.md` §4).
