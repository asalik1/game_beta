# Gear Flavor — Warlock

The pact-bound curse-wielder (he/him): blood, hexes, the void, power bought at a price. His
gear speaks in the language of debt and contract — creditors, clauses, ledgers, the far side of
the membrane leaning in — and of the god-kings the Concord's seals were raised to hold back.
Kit context: Soulthirst (life on damage, magic penetration), Shadowbolt, Hex (wither, expose,
detonate-on-death, deepens while held), Dark Pact (spend max HP for a soul-drain blast and a
lifesteal surge), Void Rift (drag-and-burst, always crits the cursed).
