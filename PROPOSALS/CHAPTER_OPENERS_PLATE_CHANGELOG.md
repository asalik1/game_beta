asset generation started (an owner dedupe review, then an owner plate-pose

the 97 plates are stale**. This file is the exact regenerate/keep list —

**back up any already-generated plate you replace, never delete.**

from here on): *plates are scenes, not emblems* — no restaged class
signature poses (mage laying-on-hands, warrior staring at hands, archer
thread-vision wide shot, paladin chain-as-prop, warlock tome closeup)
