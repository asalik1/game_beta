# Stillwater fishing

A catch-and-release activity between fights, available to every class. Visit
**Stillwater Reach near Emberfall** (a village branch in the seeded map), or
look for the tackle box and fishing rod beside a marsh or bog river bridge.
Interact with the nook to start. The Catch journal lives in Codex → World.

Choose a free lure, cast, and wait for the float to dip. Strike on **BITE**,
then hold **Space** or the **Reel** button to bring the fish in. A controller's
**RT** (R2) also casts, strikes and holds the reel. Release when
the fish warns of a surge and while it runs. The upper meter is landing
progress; the lower meter is line tension. Holding continuously snaps the
line, while never reeling lets the fish escape. Neither failure costs items.
An early strike misses; a quick correct strike and controlled tension improve
the catch's size.

Four original species have different size ranges and resistance:

| Fish | Favoured lure | Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| Greyrun Dace | Reed fly | Clear river |
| Copperfin | Copper spinner | Clear river |
| Glass Eel | Moon feather | Dark marsh/bog river |
| Crown Koi | Moon feather | Rare in either |

All four remain possible with any lure in either habitat. Every landed fish
records its species, count and personal best length, then swims free. The
first catch awards **A Quiet Moment**; collecting all four awards
**Riverkeeper** and unlocks **the Riverkeeper** title in Codex → Records.
Records travel with the character through saves, chapter replays and guest
take-home saves. Fishing creates no sellable items, currency or combat power.

Solo fishing pauses the world. Co-op uses the ordinary live menu policy:
nearby enemies prevent casting, and damage, death, leaving the nook, a room
change or closing/replacing the panel cancels it. Each angler has a personal
journal; there is no shared shoal claim or new network message. A closing
panel cannot record a late catch. Focus loss releases held input and suspends
the local minigame.
Build 0.3.5 uses the existing join-version check to keep the added campaign
room consistent between peers.

Implementation:

- `scripts/fishing.gd`: seeded state machine, catch tables, claim-once records,
  save sanitization and safety check. Tuning is in `balance.gd`.
- `scripts/fishing_spot.gd`: original prop, animated shoal, existing keyboard/
  touch interaction registry, scenery lifecycle cleanup.
- `scripts/ui/fishing.gd`: animated water, live float/line/meters, mouse/touch/
  keyboard controls and illustrated Codex journal.
- `scripts/content/fishing_bank.gd`: appended village branch. Existing authored
  room indices and weekly breach slots are retained; compass directions follow
  the run's seeded map.
- Two generated source assets, used directly with mipmaps and linear filtering.
  Fish use `AtlasTexture` regions; no raster extraction or resampling step.
  Prompts and provenance: `art_src/fishing_2026-09-07/README.md`.
