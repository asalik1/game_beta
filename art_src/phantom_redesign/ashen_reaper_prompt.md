> Use case: stylized-concept

> Asset type: static pixel-art player-character sprite concept for Crownless

> Primary request: Create a new original Mythic Assassin concept: an ashen

>
> Input images and roles:
> - Image 1 is an emotional and silhouette reference only. Capture its gravity,
>   patience, oppressive darkness, skull recessed in shadow, concentrated ember

>   dual-dagger identity. Do not copy its hood, armor, cyan fire, pose, cape, or

>   rendering language: compact full-body proportions, strong readable
>   silhouette, hard pixel clusters, crisp dark outline, restrained
>   limited-palette shading. Do not copy its samurai design or colors.
>
> Character design:
> - One male player character, front/south-facing idle stance, full body visible.
> - His posture is relaxed but predatory: shoulders slightly forward, head
>   lowered, ember eyes staring upward, both arms hanging low with effortless
>   control. He must look ancient and sovereign, not like a shambling mob or
>   combat mannequin.
> - Exposed skull face made of aged, smoke-darkened bone. The upper skull is
>   charred nearly black, with a few narrow molten-orange cracks. Deep black
>   sockets contain tiny concentrated ember-red eyes. The skull is menacing and
>   intelligent, not grinning comically.
> - He must NOT look bald. A dense mane/cowl of slow black flame and ash grows
>   from both temples, the full crown, and rear cranium, wrapping and partly
>   obscuring the skull dome. The outer silhouette is mostly soot-black
>   smoke-flame with subtle dark-crimson edges and only a few dim orange embers
>   within. It should resemble heavy supernatural hair made from ash and fire,
>   swept backward and downward—not tall spiky anime hair, not a small candle
>   flame, and not a cloth hood.
> - A broad, heavy, ragged shoulder mantle creates a top-heavy triangular
>   silhouette. It is made from layered black burial cloth with burnt, irregular
>   edges. Below the shoulders the silhouette narrows into a lean, mobile
>   Assassin body.
> - Asymmetric layered half-coat and burial-cloth panels taper behind him,
>   conveying age and weight without hiding the two solid, clearly animatable
>   legs and fitted boots.
> - Under the mantle is restrained fitted black armor with one small iron
>   demon-binding seal embedded at the sternum, glowing very faintly like a
>   banked coal. No exposed rib-cage costume.
> - Exactly two short hooked daggers, one in each hand, held low in reverse grip.
>   Dark blackened-bone blades with only a narrow burnt-orange edge. Compact
>   Assassin weapons, not swords, not sickles.
> - No large flames elsewhere. The idle form is mostly black and ash; the eyes,
>   skull cracks, sternum seal, and dagger edges provide the only small orange-red
>   accents. This restraint must leave visual room for later dash and ultimate
>   ignition.
> - Color palette: soot black, charcoal, deep brown-black, muted iron,
>   smoke-dark aged bone, dark crimson, burnt orange, tiny white-hot ember cores.
>   Absolutely no cyan, teal, blue fire, or gold ornament.
> - Overall read: a playable Mythic Assassin who embodies death itself—calm,
>   regal, predatory, and unmistakably more important than a normal enemy.
