## Shared call structure

All four calls used the Crownless pants contract: one complete connected pair
of empty legguards, orthographic front three-quarter catalogue view, centered
at roughly 82% of a square, crisp high-detail sprite illustration readable at
128px and 32px, upper-left light, and a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma
background. Every call prohibited a wearer, body, mannequin, feet, boots,
weapons, detached parts, scenery, floor, shadows, readable text, UI framing,
watermarks, and use of the key color in the item.
