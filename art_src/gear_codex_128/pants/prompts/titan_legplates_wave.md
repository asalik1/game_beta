The B/A/S calls used the preceding accepted family image as a visual reference.

## `p_titan_legplates`

Neutral colossal warrior leg armor: joined heavy charcoal gambeson trousers
carrying broad overlapping battered dark-iron thigh slabs, thick squared knee
cops, massive articulated shin guards, a wide iron-and-leather waist defense,
dark leather joints, dull rivets, and open empty ankle cuffs. Practical,
weight-bearing titan armor with no magical glow.

## Shared generation constraints

Every call used the Crownless dual-resolution stylized-concept contract:
exactly one connected standalone pants object, orthographic front three-quarter
catalogue view, centered at roughly 82% of a square, crisp high-detail sprite
illustration readable at 128px and 32px, upper-left light, and a perfectly flat
solid chroma field (`#00ff00` for neutral/B, `#ff00ff` for A/S/uniques).
All calls prohibited a floor, shadow, wearer, body, mannequin, feet, boots,
shoes, soles, weapon, detached parts or VFX, scenery, readable text, UI framing,
watermark, duplicate object, smoke, blur, and use of the key color in the item.
