## Orchestrator corrections (verified against the engine, 2026-09-03)

**CLIPSCALE numbers in the mob audits used the wrong render model.** Several
"the attack plays 0.9x/0.64x the idle body" findings normalise the clip by its
OWN cell. `enemy.gd _apply_strip` does that only for LOCOMOTION strips; an
ACTION strip whose cell is >= the idle cell renders off the IDLE reference
(`ref = _body_cell`), so its on-screen body ratio is simply
`clip_body / idle_body` — the cell size drops out. Re-measured:

| clip | audit claim | real ratio | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| null_acolyte_attack | 0.93x, "downscale" | **1.07x** | fine, no edit |
| vale_mourner_attack | 0.93x, "downscale" | **1.07x** | fine, no edit |
| greyrun_lurker_attack | 1.12x up | **0.86x** | borderline, left |
| static_caller_death | 0.58x | 0.58x but it is a COLLAPSE pose | correct by design |
| storm_harrier_walk | — | **0.84x** (locomotion, real) | REGEN (queued) |
| halla_walk_codex_e | rescale | **0.95x** | marginal, left |

Do not apply the "uniform downscale about the feet" edits those rows asked for;
they would shrink clips that already render correctly.

**Green keying ring — corpus sweep (new, not in any audit).** A rim-band scan
(`tools/art/despill_rim.py --scan`) found **66 sprites** ringed with the
ImageGen `#00FF00` matte (38-61% of the silhouette rim green-dominant, interior
~0 = halo not design). 63 fixed. This includes **19 WARLOCK attack/attack2
strips** — CLAUDE.md's 2026-08-23 BLEED triage concluded "the green rim is
absent on base heroes", but that investigation covered cast/ult (where the
green IS FX content); the warlock's `attack`/`attack2` family carries a real
ring around a design with no green in it at all. Verified on a grass composite
plus a rim/interior mask overlay before touching a hero sprite.
Skipped as genuine content: `canopy_forest` (foliage), `wall_sewer` (moss tile).
