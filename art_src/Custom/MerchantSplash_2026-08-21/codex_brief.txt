The dialogue/shop splash portrait for the traveling merchant of "Crownless",
a painterly dark-fantasy action RPG. It must sit beside 165 sibling splashes:
style_sibling_splash.png (attached) is one of them — match its rendering
style, level of finish, palette discipline and dramatic lighting EXACTLY.
Painterly digital painting, muted desaturated palette, moody atmosphere,
soft-shaded, NO black comic outlines, no cel shading.
