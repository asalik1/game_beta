WHAT THIS IS
The bag/socket icon for the Opal gem in "Crownless", a painterly
dark-fantasy RPG. It renders at ~32px, so it MUST stay crisp when shrunk (the
old gems failed here — soft gradients turn to mush). identity_old_cut.png is
the colour/subject reference; redraw BOLDER and HARDER-EDGED with a DISTINCT
cut so it isn't confused with other gems of a similar colour.

SUBJECT
- ONE Opal: a smooth oval CABOCHON with soft iridescent opal shimmer (hints of pastel colour), luminous not bright, in pale milky blue-white. Centred, upright, filling ~80% of the frame,
  face-on with a slight top-down tilt.

RENDERING RULES (beat the mush)
- BOLD FLAT FACETS, HARD edges between them. NO soft airbrush gradient, NO
  blur, NO photoreal gloss.
- Limited palette: ~4-5 stepped tones dark-core-to-bright-edge, plus ONE small
  white specular glint upper-left (light top-left). MUTED, jewel-like, not neon.
- A clear darker rim so the silhouette reads at tiny size.

BACKGROUND / CONSTRAINTS
- Solid pure magenta #FF00FF behind the gem (chroma key). No magenta spill, no
  glow, no vignette, no metal setting, no ring, no text, no shadow.
